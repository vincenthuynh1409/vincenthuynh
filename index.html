01 / About

Background

I got into security the way a lot of people do — by breaking something I wasn't supposed to, then getting curious about why it worked. That curiosity turned into a career spent on both sides of the fence: red-teaming production applications during the day, and studying how real intrusions unfold at night.

Today I work as a penetration tester and security analyst, focused on web application security, cloud misconfigurations, and adversary simulation. I care less about collecting findings and more about writing reports engineers actually want to read — clear reproduction steps, real business impact, and fixes that don't just move the risk somewhere else.

Outside of client work, I compete in CTFs, contribute to open-source security tooling, and write up interesting vulnerabilities I stumble across along the way.
